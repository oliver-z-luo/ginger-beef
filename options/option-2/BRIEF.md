# Brief: Option 2 · Bright Counter

## Scope and mode
Persuade, with an Operate-style menu and bag. The one-page site covers the promo carousel, smorgasbord and family dinner tiles, a tabbed and searchable menu, combo builder, family dinners, smorgasbord and visit info.

## Direction
**THESIS:** A bright, friendly, quick-service-style ordering site: clean white, one confident red (#C8202A), rounded everything. The visitor gets from a promo to a dish in the bag fast.

**WORLD:** White ground with soft blush (#FDF0EE) panels and ink #1D1D1F text. Figtree at 800–900 weight for headings. Pill buttons and chips. Cards have 22–28px corners. A dark utility bar shows the live open status, and a sticky white header carries the red wordmark, a call pill and a bag button with a count.

**STORY:** The visitor sees a new-dish promo and the $25 smorgasbord and family dinner tiles. They use the menu tabs, or search by name or number, add dishes to the bag, open the bag (a right drawer on desktop, a bottom sheet on mobile), then call.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** On the left, a large blush promo carousel (N1–N3) with heading, description, price, an "Add to bag" pill, a photo with a "New" badge, plus arrows and dots. On the right, two photo tiles: Smorgasbord $25 and Family Dinners from $41.95.

**SIGNATURE INTERACTION:** Add buttons turn ink and show "n in bag", and the bag icon bumps. The carousel crossfades with a slight photo scale-settle, advances every 7 seconds, and pauses on hover, focus, or reduced motion.

## Constraints
- No checkout; the phone is the way to order. Prices are before GST.
- Stand-in photos and the invented new dishes (N1–N3) are labelled as such.
- Never name or imitate another restaurant or brand.
