# Brief: Option 1 · Storefront

## Scope and mode
Persuade. The one-page site covers the hero, favourites, new dishes, smorgasbord, the full menu with cart, combo, family dinners and visit info. The menu and cart work like an app inside it.

## Direction
**THESIS:** A bold, photo-led local restaurant site that shows the buffet offer, a menu you can actually order from, and how to call. It's the familiar restaurant-site arrangement, done at the craft level of a polished independent restaurant.

**WORLD:** White page with black and red (#D42A24) feature blocks. Anton uppercase condensed display type, Archivo for body text. Photos have 14px corners and buttons 8px. A floating white nav pill sits over a full-bleed photo hero. A red marquee band runs below the favourites, followed by alternating 50/50 photo and text splits.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the $25 smorgasbord and whether it's open tonight. They browse favourites or the full numbered menu (1–110), add dishes, open the slide-out order with GST and pickup-discount math, then call.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed buffet photo. The uppercase headline "All-you-can-eat dinner, $25 a person" sits bottom-left, with the price on a red block. Below it: a live open/closed pill, hours and address, and the CTAs "Order from the menu" and the phone number. The floating nav sits top-right with a bag count and a red "Call to order".

**SIGNATURE INTERACTION:** + buttons turn red and show the quantity, and the floating order pill bumps. The new-dishes split wipes its photo across (clip-path) when a dish is hovered or selected.

## Constraints
- No checkout; the phone is the way to order. Prices are before GST.
- Stand-in photos and the invented new dishes (N1–N3) are labelled as such.
- Never name or imitate another restaurant or brand.
